Who runs this

Audit with Proof is a one-person, independent audit practice. It is operated by the owner of this repository, reachable through the contact details in the proforma (payment-proforma.md).

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising cookie and no third-party script on these pages. The site is static files served by GitHub Pages.

What the audit itself handles

  • Your artefact. Static analysis only. The software you submit is never executed, and it is deleted after delivery unless you ask us to keep it in writing.
  • Credentials. We never ask for, store or request your Google, GitHub or social account passwords. Session material, where a platform requires it, is held encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and never appears in logs, dry-run output or this repository.
  • Reports. The report and its evidence bundle belong to the client. We do not publish a client's report, findings or name without written permission. The public sample in this repository is an audit of a public, open-source application (draw.io Desktop, GPL-3.0), analysed under the terms of its own licence.
